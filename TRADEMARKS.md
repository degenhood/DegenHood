# DegenHood brand policy

> **Draft for counsel review.** Nothing here claims a registration that has not been obtained.

`DegenHood`, `$DEGEN`, `HOOD STAYS ON`, associated logos, character designs, pack artwork and the
distinctive DegenHood visual identity are controlled by the applicable DegenHood rights holders to
the extent protected by applicable law.

Open-source and documentation licences in this repository do not grant trademark or brand rights.

## Generally acceptable

- accurate, non-misleading reference to DegenHood or compatibility with its public interfaces;
- factual commentary, news, criticism and comparison;
- links to the official website, contracts or repository;
- unmodified screenshots used to explain genuine interaction, subject to applicable law.

## Permission required

- using a DegenHood mark, logo or character as part of a product, company, token, domain, handle or
  repository name;
- merchandising, commercial artwork or derivative character/pack assets;
- presenting a fork, deployment, service or token as official, approved or endorsed;
- copying DegenHood trade dress in a way likely to create confusion;
- using brand assets in token promotion, fundraising or financial advertising.

Do not use the marks in a way that is deceptive, defamatory, unlawful or likely to confuse users
about source, sponsorship or affiliation.

## No endorsement

Reference implementations, forks and integrations must identify their own operator and contract
addresses prominently. Compatibility does not imply endorsement. Full contract address and chain ID
take precedence over a token name, ticker, logo or website label.

## Requests and reports

The final policy should provide an approved role address for brand permissions and impersonation
reports. Do not publish a personal address or an unmonitored inbox.
