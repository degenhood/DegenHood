# Licensing

> **Publication policy.** Final ownership notices and brand terms require counsel review before the
> first public release.

This is a mixed-license repository. No single root licence applies to every file.

## Licence map

| Material | Terms | Reason |
| --- | --- | --- |
| All first-party public software, including LaunchHub, v4, Degenetics and SDK/CLI | MIT | A programmable system open to forks and public contributions |
| `openapi` and technical documentation | CC BY 4.0 | Supports citation, diligence and implementation documentation |
| Brand assets: names, logos, character art, pack art and visual identity | All rights reserved; trademark policy applies | Software licences do not grant brand rights |
| Third-party material | Its original licence | Preserve notices and attribution exactly; see `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md` |

The published repository contains exact standard texts under `LICENSES/` and a machine-readable
`REUSE.toml`. That file is authoritative when a path-specific rule differs from the root software
licence. Brand terms remain separate under `LicenseRef-DegenHood-Brand`; this repository does not
include the production product assets.

Public dependencies retain their own licences and notices as recorded in
[`THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md`](THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md). The first-party MIT licence does not override
those terms.

## Copyright notice

The repository currently uses the collective notice `DegenHood contributors` for public software
and documentation. Before the first public release, counsel should confirm whether that should be
replaced with a legal person or entity. The recommended form after that review is:

```text
Copyright © 2026 [COPYRIGHT OWNER]. All rights reserved except as expressly licensed.
```

Existing notices must remain intact. Confirm that employment, contractor and contributor agreements
actually assign or license the necessary rights to the named owner before making the statement.

## What publication does not grant

MIT permits broad software reuse subject to its notice and disclaimer. Neither MIT nor CC BY grants:

- rights in DegenHood names, marks, logos, artwork or trade dress;
- permission to imply endorsement, affiliation or official status;
- rights in third-party protocols, marks or assets;
- any patent licence beyond one expressly stated in the applicable licence;
- access to private infrastructure or production services.

## Contribution policy

All code contributions use Developer Certificate of Origin sign-off and are accepted under MIT.
Documentation contributions are accepted under CC BY 4.0. A contributor must have the right to
submit the material; issue reports and ideas do not themselves transfer ownership.

## Practical protection outside the repository

Repository notices are only one layer. The owner should separately consider with qualified counsel:

- copyright registration for significant software releases and original visual works in relevant
  jurisdictions;
- trademark clearance and registration for the DegenHood word mark and core logos in the actual
  goods/services classes used;
- written IP assignment from founders, employees and contractors;
- a brand-asset register with creator, source file, creation date and assignment evidence;
- third-party licence inventory, SBOM generation and periodic provenance review;
- takedown, impersonation and counterfeit-response procedures.

This document is a technical publication recommendation, not legal advice.
